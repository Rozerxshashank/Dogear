Connect to your Mac

Your notes stay on your Mac. This phone reads them over your Wi-Fi.

  1. On your Mac, open SmartNotes → Settings → Phone.
  2. Scan the QR code with your camera, or type the code shown under it.

Works when your Mac is awake and on the same Wi-Fi. Use it on networks you trust, like home: traffic on the Wi-Fi isn't encrypted.
