Markdown notes app for Mac

Notes that find themselves.

SmartNotes keeps everything you write as ordinary Markdown files on your Mac, then lets you search them by what you meant instead of the exact words you used. No account, no subscription, and it works offline.

You remember the idea, not the wording.

Six months from now you won't recall whether you wrote "focus", "distraction" or "deep work". You'll remember roughly what it was about. So that's how SmartNotes searches.

It reads every note for meaning, then combines that with ordinary keyword search. All of it runs on your Mac, with no internet connection and nothing extra to install.

Your notes are files. That's the point.

Every note is a .md file in Documents/SmartNotes, in the folders you'd expect. Open them in any editor, sync them however you like, or stop using SmartNotes tomorrow and keep everything.

New in 1.1

Your notes on your phone. No cloud.

Most apps that keep notes on your computer make you pay for sync, or hand your notes to someone's server. SmartNotes skips both: your phone talks straight to your Mac over your home Wi-Fi.

  1. In SmartNotes on your Mac, open Settings → Phone and turn it on.
  2. Point your phone's camera at the QR code. That's the whole setup. No app to install, no account.
  3. Read, search and write notes from your phone. Changes show up on your Mac straight away.

Works while your Mac is awake and on the same Wi-Fi. Phones can't delete notes, and an edit never overwrites unsaved changes on your Mac.

Ask your notes a question.

The chat panel answers from your notes only, and names the notes it used so you can check. If the answer isn't in what you wrote, it says so rather than making something up.

Run it entirely on your Mac with Ollama, or paste an OpenAI, Mistral or Gemini key in Settings. If you do neither, everything else in the app still works.

Before you switch

The things people usually want to know first.

Does it send my notes anywhere?

No. Notes, the search index and settings stay on your Mac. The only time text leaves your computer is if you add a cloud AI key and use chat, in which case the passages relevant to your question go to that provider. GitHub backup only runs when you press Push. Phone access, if you turn it on, goes directly between your phone and your Mac on your own Wi-Fi.

What happens if I stop using SmartNotes?

Nothing happens to your notes. They're Markdown files in a normal folder. Delete the app and they're still there, readable by any text editor, Obsidian, iA Writer or whatever you move to next.

Why does macOS warn me the first time I open it?

This early version isn't notarized by Apple yet, which costs a paid developer account we haven't set up. macOS shows a warning for any app in that state.

To open it: drag SmartNotes into Applications, then right-click it and choose Open. If macOS still refuses, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.

Do I need Ollama?

No. Search, linking and the editor work out of the box. Ollama is only needed if you want chat to run on your Mac instead of through a cloud provider. If you install it, pull two models:

ollama pull llama3.2 and ollama pull nomic-embed-text

Is there a version for Intel Macs or Windows?

Not yet. This release is built for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer). Windows and Intel builds are planned; open an issue if you'd use one, so we know to prioritise it.

Can I use it with Obsidian?

Yes. Import a vault in Settings and its folders and [[wikilinks]] come across as they are. Or keep both: open Documents/SmartNotes as a vault in Obsidian and both apps work on the same Markdown files. Changes made in one show up in the other.

Is there a phone app?

You don't need one. Turn on Settings → Phone on your Mac and scan the QR code: your phone opens SmartNotes in its browser, and you can add it to your home screen so it works like an app.

It works while your Mac is awake and on the same Wi-Fi. Traffic on your Wi-Fi isn't encrypted, so use it on networks you trust, like home. You can disconnect a phone from Settings at any time.

Is it really free?

Yes. It's open source, there's no account and nothing to upgrade to. If you use a cloud AI provider, that provider bills you directly for what you use.

Try it with the notes you already have.

Point it at an existing folder of Markdown or an Obsidian vault and see what it finds. It takes about a minute.
